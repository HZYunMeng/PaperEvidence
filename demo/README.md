# Demo asset attribution

`evidence-view.png`, `semantic-evidence-view.png` and `cell-citation-view.png` show the project-authored fictional fixture; the values are invented for software tests.

`real-cell-citation-view.png`, `real-value-lookup.jpg` and `real-value-source.jpg` are actual application screenshots. Its source table and PDF preview derive from Radford et al. (2021), **Learning Transferable Visual Models From Natural Language Supervision**, PMLR 139, pp. 8748–8763. [Official paper and author list](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/radford21a.html). The original paper is distributed under CC-BY-4.0 under the [PMLR publication agreement](https://proceedings.mlr.press/pmlr-license-agreement.html). Source copyright belongs to its authors. The app adds yellow highlight overlays and extracts text into its interface.

`real-cell-citation.json` records the inspected source cell and coordinates, not an AI-generated research result. The short source caption retains the paper's wording. These source-derived assets retain their source attribution and license; the repository's software license does not replace them.
